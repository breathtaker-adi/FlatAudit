One safety rule I added

If the app is not sure a page is rent or sale, it shows no price comparison until the user picks. Without this, one wrong guess gives a wrong comparison.

About “is this place worth it?”

The app won’t give one big “worth it” verdict. The price check says how the rate compares. The Area Report says what is nearby. The user puts them together. This matches your “facts, not verdicts” rule.

Risks to remember

Map gaps

  • Small areas may have missing shops or bus stops
  • A missing place can look like a real drawback, so say “not found on the map”

Few listings

  • Early on, many areas will say “not enough listings”
  • That is correct, not a bug

Correctness work, later

  • Test rent/sale detection on 100+ real pages per portal
  • Check each official data record against its source
  • Re-run the price spike simulation with more areas
  • Remove the localStorage fallback in the extension

Do these before inviting beta users. Both new features depend on them.
