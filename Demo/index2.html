    Benchmarks without hardcoding

    The every-city fallback ladder

    No rigid rupee thresholds. Every rent is scored against the strongest evidence available — falling back gracefully, and staying silent rather than guessing.

    2. L1

      Peer page n ≥ 6

      Search-result cards and “similar properties” on the very page you’re reading. Works day one, in any Indian city, with zero server dependency.

    3. L2

      Area cell n ≥ 10

      Fine geohash cell (~1–5 km) or locality pack, aggregated anonymously from the hash index.

    4. L3

      District cell n ≥ 8

      Parent geohash prefix (~20–40 km) when the locality itself is too thin.

    5. L4

      City baseline n ≥ 5

      Metro aggregate by BHK — the last rung before silence.

    6. L5

      Silence mode n < 5

      Fewer than five comparable observations? Price flags are suppressed entirely. Non-price signals — advance-fee phrases, stolen photos, zero-deposit traps — keep working.

    Robust statistics

    Median & MAD, not averages

    z = 0.6745 × (x − median) / MAD
    • One ₹90,000 outlier can’t poison a median.
    • Trimmed aggregation happens server-side on anonymous primitives only.
    • Packs cached with ETag → 304 Not Modified, no compute per visit.

      How it works

      • Strictly opt-in. Toggling it on requests Chrome’s optional host permission for overpass-api.de.
      • ~100 m grid. Coordinates rounded to 3 decimals before querying.
      • 14-day TTL + 50-entry LRU cache in chrome.storage.local — repeat visits never re-query.
      • City-tier tuned. Metro vs tier-2/3 thresholds — see the toggle below.
      • Neutral map facts only. No subjective “good/bad area” wording — distances and grades.

      City-tier thresholds

      • Emergency healthcare · grade A ≤1.5 km
      • Daily supplies · grade A ≤400 m
      • Commute · grade A ≤0.8 km
      • Park (safety/env) · grade A ≤500 m
      • Lifestyle cluster · grade A ≤600 m

      Same OSM data, different cut-offs — so a “B” in Jodhpur isn’t unfairly a “C”.

      Grade legend

      AGood — amenities well within threshold
      BOkay — present, a little further
      CWeak — sparse or distant
      DNone observed on the map
      05 — Privacy by design

      Trust that never asks for your data

      • No accounts, no phone numbers, no emails. There is nothing to leak.
      • Listing photos are hashed locally to a 64-bit fingerprint — raw pixels are never uploaded, cached, or stored.
      • No listing URLs in the index — only anonymous structural primitives: BHK, rent, geohash, hash, day.
      • innerHTML is forbidden across all production scripts — DOM inputs are sanitised via textContent.
      • Telemetry is opt-in and anonymous — a random UUID, verdict labels, nothing else.
